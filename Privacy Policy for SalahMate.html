1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how JHMobileApps ("JHMobileApps", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the SalahMate Android application and its related web pages (together, "SalahMate" or the "App"). For the purposes of data protection law, JHMobileApps is the controller of the personal information described here, except where a third party acts as an independent controller under its own policy, as explained in Section 6.

SalahMate provides prayer times, the Adhan, Quran reading and recitation, Adhkar, Duas, Hadith, a tasbih counter, a Hijri calendar, the Qibla direction, prayer and fasting tracking, notifications, home-screen widgets, optional accounts with cloud synchronization, coins and rewards, advertising, and optional purchases.

This policy does not cover third-party websites, services, or content that SalahMate links to or uses, which are governed by their own terms and policies. By using SalahMate, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where the law requires your consent for a particular use, we rely on that consent only for that use, and you may withdraw it at any time, as described below.

2. Location

How location is used. With your permission, SalahMate reads your device's location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction on your device. JHMobileApps does not receive, store on its servers, sell, or share your GPS coordinates, and does not pass them to advertisers.

Background location. If you choose "Allow all the time" in Android settings, SalahMate may read your location while the App is closed, solely to keep your prayer times and Adhan alarms correct when you travel to another city. This location is processed on your device in the same way and is never uploaded, shared, or used for advertising. Background location is never required: with "Allow only while using the app", SalahMate updates your city when you open it.

The resulting city or region and the calculation settings are saved on your device so prayer times keep working offline. You can choose a city manually instead of granting location permission, and you can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings. Without it, automatic prayer times and the Qibla compass may be less accurate.

Separately from SalahMate's own use of location, Google's advertising services may infer an approximate location (for example, country or city) from your IP address, as described in Section 7.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information that stays on your device

Without an account, SalahMate stores your settings, location choice, prayer and fasting records, Quran bookmarks and reading position, Adhkar and tasbih progress, and downloaded recitations locally on your device. JHMobileApps cannot access this data. It is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3.2 Information you give us when you create an account

Account details

Email address, account identifier, display name and profile picture (if provided, for example by Google Sign-In), preferred language, and account timestamps. Passwords are handled by Supabase Authentication in hashed form, and JHMobileApps cannot read them.

Synced app data

Notification and Adhan settings, Quran reading position and pages read, prayer completion records, fasting records, Adhkar and tasbih activity, custom tasbih phrases, display preferences, and the days on which you opened the App.

Coins, rewards and unlocks

Coin balance and transaction history, chests, badges, achievements, streaks, and the reciters or items you have unlocked.

Purchases

Product identifier, purchase token, order or transaction identifier, purchase time, verification status, and the resulting entitlement. We never receive your full payment card details.

Rewarded ad records

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, a one-time reference, the ad unit, a transaction identifier issued by Google, the reward, and timestamps, used to confirm that the ad was completed before a reward is granted.

Support messages

The content of any email you send us and your email address, used only to answer you.

3.3 Information collected automatically

Our hosting and service providers automatically process technical information that is needed to run, secure, and deliver the service, such as IP address, request timestamps, device and operating-system type, App version, and error information in server logs. SalahMate does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or any other third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The advertising SDK described in Section 7 collects its own information.

We do not intentionally collect government identifiers, precise location on our servers, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input, or health information other than the religious practice data described in Section 4.

4. Religious Practice Data

Records of your prayers, fasting, Quran reading, and dhikr may reveal your religious beliefs. Some laws, including the EU and UK GDPR, treat this as a special category of personal data, and others treat it as sensitive personal information.

  • These records stay on your device unless you create an account and sign in. Signing in and using sync is your explicit choice to have them stored in your account so they are available across devices and count toward rewards.
  • We use them only to provide the features you use (sync, streaks, statistics, achievements, and rewards). We do not use them for advertising or profiling, we do not sell or share them for advertising, and we do not pass them to advertising partners.
  • You can withdraw this consent at any time by signing out (records then stay on your device only) or by deleting your account, as described in Section 11. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.

  • Consent: where the law requires consent for ads (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), ads are served according to the consent choices made through Google's consent tools, and you can change those choices at any time.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose whether to watch one.

10. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes in this policy:

  • Account and synced app data: while your account is active, and then until it is deleted as described in Section 11.
  • Purchase records: for as long as needed to provide your entitlements and to meet tax, accounting, consumer-protection, and dispute-resolution requirements. This is generally up to the period required by applicable law, after which they are deleted or anonymized.
  • Rewarded ad and coin records: for as long as your account exists, to prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards.
  • Server and security logs: kept by our providers for limited periods under their own retention schedules.
  • Support emails: for as long as needed to resolve your request and any follow-up.
  • Location: not retained by JHMobileApps.

We may retain information for longer only where the law requires it, or where it is needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. In those cases we restrict it to that purpose.

11. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the App, or by following the steps on the account deletion page. You can also email us from your account's email address.

  • After you request deletion, your account is scheduled for deletion and permanently erased after a 30-day grace period. This protects you against accidental or unauthorized requests. Signing in during that period lets you cancel the request.
  • When the period ends, your account and the data linked to it (profile, synced app data, religious practice data, coins, rewards, unlocks, and rewarded ad records) are permanently deleted from our database. Copies in our provider's encrypted backups are overwritten as those backups expire.
  • Coins, unlocked items, and other in-app benefits linked to the account are lost on deletion and cannot be restored or refunded, except where required by law. Subscriptions and purchases made through Google Play must be cancelled or refunded in Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as purchase records, are kept only as described in Section 10.
  • Data stored only on your device is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

12. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. These include encrypted connections (TLS) to our backend, password hashing by our authentication provider, row-level access rules so that each account can read only its own records, server-side verification of purchases and rewards, and restricting administrative access.

Some Quran recitation sources may not support encrypted connections. These requests contain no account information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where and as required by law. You are responsible for keeping your device and your account credentials secure.
